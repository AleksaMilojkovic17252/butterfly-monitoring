Researcher manual

How to use the Butterfly Monitoring app in the field: from your first login to a finished, synced visit.

About this manual

This manual is for people who walk butterfly transects with the Android app. You do not need any technical knowledge. If you are new, read The big picture and Getting started, then follow Walking a visit once with the app in your hand.

Later you can come back to look something up. The Messages and Problems and answers chapters explain what to do when the app tells you something unexpected.

Every screenshot comes from the real app, using an invented example project called Demo – User Manual. Your project, transects and species will differ.

How to read the boxes

CarefulSomething that can lose data or spoil a record.

Words written like Start visit are the exact labels of buttons and fields in the app.

The big picture

The system supports standardised butterfly counts called Pollard walks. You walk a fixed route, count the butterflies in a fixed box around you, and note the weather and your route. Doing it the same way every time is what makes counts from different days and places comparable.

There are two kinds of user:

  • Researchers use the phone app. They walk visits and record what they see. They see only their own records.
  • Admins use a website. They create projects and transects, add people to projects, check uncertain records, and export the data. Admins can also use the app.

The app works without a connection

Everything you need for the field is stored on your phone: the transects, the species list, the rules and your own records. Fields often have no signal, so the app never needs a connection to record anything.

When the phone is online again, the app sends your new records to the server and fetches anything new. This is called sync. It happens by itself, and you can also start it yourself. See Sync.

CarefulUntil a record has synced, the only copy is on your phone. Sync regularly, and before you uninstall the app or change phones.

Getting started

What you need

  • An Android phone with GPS, running Android 7 or newer. There is no iPhone version.
  • An account. An admin creates it for you. There is no sign-up form.
  • To be added to a project by an admin.
  • A connection for the first login and the first download. After that the app works offline.

Get your account

Ask your project admin for an account. They will give you an email address and a password to log in with. The same person adds you to your project. If you log in and see no projects, they have not done this yet.

Install the app

  1. On your phone, open the project's download page and download the app. It is an APK file, which is how Android apps are installed from outside the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android may warn you that it comes from an unknown source. Allow your browser or file manager to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Butterfly Monitoring.
For nowThe download page and the public server do not exist yet. Until they do, your admin sends you the APK file and tells you the server address. Enter that address before you log in: on the login screen tap the gear at the top right, type the address in URL, tap Test Connection, then Save. Connected (200) means it works. The server is currently a laptop, so it can only be reached the way your admin sets up for you. On some phones these two buttons sit partly behind Android's navigation bar. Tap their upper edge.
Coming laterWhen the system is finished, the server address will be built in. You will only install the app and log in.

Log in

Enter the email and password you were given and tap Log in. The eye icon shows the password as you type it.

  • You stay logged in. Next time, the app opens straight away, even without a connection. It reopens the project you used last.
  • Forgotten password: the app has no reset. Ask an admin, who can set a new one for you.
  • Wrong email or password. Check for typing mistakes. After five failed tries, that account is blocked from logging in for 15 minutes.
  • Can't reach the server. Check your connection. You have no connection, or the server is not running. Try again later, or ask your admin.

Download your project

After the first login you see Projects, the list of projects you belong to. A project must be downloaded before you can use it in the field.

  1. Find your project and tap Download. Do this somewhere with a good connection.
  2. Wait until the card shows Ready. The progress line counts the records received. Cancel pauses the download, and tapping Download again continues it.
  3. Tap the card to open the project.
Status on the cardMeaningWhat to do

OutdatedThe server has newer data for this project than this phone, for example a changed transect.Tap Update when you have a connection.

If the list says No projects on this phone yet, pull the list down to refresh it. If nothing appears, ask an admin to add you to a project.

Before each field day

Do this at home while you still have a connection.

  • Open the app and let it sync. Unsynced data should have no badge.
  • Check that your project card says Ready. If it says Outdated, tap Update.
  • Open the map once for your study area while online. Map backgrounds you have viewed stay on the phone for a while. See Maps without a connection.
  • Charge the phone. GPS uses a lot of battery, so a power bank helps on long days.
  • Check the weather limits of your project. See The field method.

The field method in short

The app records data, but the method is what makes it useful. This is the short version. The numbers below are the standard defaults, and each project can set its own. To see your project's values, open a transect and scroll to Protocol, or look at the start form.

What to count

Walk the transect at a steady pace and count every butterfly inside an imagined recording box that moves with you:

  • 2.5 m to each side of you, so 5 m wide in total
  • 5 m ahead of you
  • 5 m above you

Butterflies outside the box are not part of the count.

When to walk

RuleDefault
SeasonSet per project, typically 1 April to 30 September
Time of day10:00 to 17:00 local time
Temperature and cloudAt least 13 °C with at most 40 % cloud, or at least 17 °C with any cloud
WindAt most 5 on the Beaufort scale
RainNone
Visits per transect8 per season

The app warns you when you are outside these limits, but it never stops you. A walk outside the limits is still saved. It is marked as not weather-suitable so that analysts can decide what to do with it.

How to record

  • One record per species per sighting. Ten individuals flying up together are one record with a count of 10. The same species 30 m further on is a new record.
  • Do not add everything up at the end. Each record has its own time and place, so enter it when you see it.
  • Record uncertainty. If you are not sure, say so with Identification confidence. See Add details.
  • Pause for anything that is not walking. Netting, identifying, photographing or resting are pauses. Paused time does not count as walking time.

What makes a visit valid

A visit counts as valid for analysis when all three of these are true:

  1. It was finished, not cancelled.
  2. The weather was within the limits, or a reason is recorded.
  3. The GPS route covers at least 80 % of the transect.

Other visits are still stored. Nothing is thrown away, so always record what really happened.

Good to knowA walk with no butterflies is valuable. Knowing a species was absent is real data. Finish the visit and confirm the true zero. Do not cancel it. See Confirm a true zero.

Transects

Open Transects on the project home screen.

The list

  • Search by name or code in the box at the top.
  • Sort by Code or Distance.
  • The location icon at the top right takes a fresh GPS position and updates the distances. Use it if the distances look wrong. Without a position they show a dash.
  • The cloud icon on each row shows whether the download of the project has finished.

The detail page

Tap a transect to see everything about it.

Tap a row in My visits to open it. The button at the bottom says Start visit, or Continue draft if you saved a draft for this transect. If an admin has made the transect inactive, the button is greyed out and the page says This transect is inactive and cannot be walked.

Good to knowYou cannot change a transect on the phone. If a route crosses private land or has become impassable, cancel the visit with the reason Invalid (protocol not followed), explain in the note, and tell your admin.
Coming laterRoute proposals will let you record a better route from the field. An admin can then turn it into a transect.

Walking a visit

This is the main job of the app. A visit has five steps:

  1. Open the start form for the transect you are standing at.
  2. Fill in the weather and tap Start. GPS recording begins.
  3. Walk and record each butterfly as you see it.
  4. Finish: check the summary and fill in the weather at the end.
  5. Sync happens by itself when you are back online.

Open the start form

Tap the Start visit tile. The app works out what you most likely want:

  • If a visit is in progress, it opens that visit.
  • If you have saved drafts in this project, it offers them, along with Choose a transect.
  • Otherwise it opens the transect list with the nearest transect first.

Tap the transect, then tap Start visit at the bottom of its page.

Good to knowThe season and time checks use your phone's time zone, which is right as long as you are at the study site.

As soon as temperature and wind are filled in, a card shows the result of the weather check.

The buttons at the bottom and top of the form:

  • Start begins the visit.
  • Save draft stores what you typed without starting. Nothing is required. Next time, the form opens with Carrying on with your draft.
  • Not conducted… at the top right records that a planned visit did not happen. See Record a visit that did not happen.

Only one visit can be in progress at a time. If another one is running, the app tells you A visit is already in progress. Finish or cancel that one first.

The first time you tap Start, the app asks for location and notification permission, and once for the battery exception. See Permissions.

During the walk

  • Time: walking time, without pauses.
  • Distance: the length of your GPS route so far.
  • Section: the section you are in. It changes by itself as you walk. A dash means you are too far from the transect.
  • Observations: how many records you have saved in this visit.
  • The map: each section in its own colour, the current one thicker. The circle around your position shows how accurate the GPS is. The dot turns grey when the position may be old, for example during a pause.

You can lock the phone

GPS recording runs in the background, so you can lock the phone and put it in your pocket. While it records, Android shows a permanent notification, Visit in progress, that you cannot swipe away. This is normal.

You can also leave the visit screen with the back arrow and look at other screens. The recording continues, and a card shows Visit in progress. Tap the card or the Start visit tile to return.

If the app is closed or the phone restarts during a visit, open the app again. The visit continues, and a line says Tracking interrupted with the times of the gap.

The two icons at the top right

  • Battery saver (battery icon) records a point every 15 seconds instead of every 5, by default. The app turns it on by itself below 20 % battery and tells you. Tap the icon to turn it on or off. The route becomes rougher, so use it only when you need to.
  • Keep the screen on (sun icon) stops the screen going dark while this screen is open.

Pause and resume

Tap Pause whenever you stop walking the transect, for example to net or identify a butterfly, take a careful photo, or rest. The timer stops and no route is recorded. Tap Resume to continue.

TipA butterfly you are netting is usually still the one you saw from the transect. Record it first, then pause while you handle it.

If GPS has a problem

A line appears above the map. The visit is not lost. Fix the cause if you can and carry on.

Record a butterfly

Tap the big Add butterfly button. A quick record takes three taps: the species, perhaps the count, and Save.

  1. Choose the species. Tap it under Recent or Favourites, or type in Search a species. Tap a star to keep a species in Favourites.
  2. Set the count. It starts at 1. Use − and +. For a large number, press and hold the number and type it (up to 9999).
  3. Tap Save. The sheet closes and a message appears at the bottom.

The app fills in the rest by itself:

  • Time: the moment you tapped Save.
  • Position: your GPS position at that moment. If no fix is available yet, the top line says No GPS fix yet – Save will get one and Save waits for one. If it cannot get one, the sheet stays open and tells you why.
  • Section: worked out from your position. If you are too far from the transect, it stays empty.
  • Accuracy: the top line shows it. If it is worse than the project's limit (25 m by default) you see Low GPS accuracy. The record is still saved.
CarefulAfter the 10 seconds of Undo, the app has no delete button for a butterfly record. You can still correct it. See Correct a record. If it should not exist at all, ask an admin to remove it.

Flight direction

Tap Flight direction and tap or drag on the dial. The blue arrow shows the way you are facing, from the phone's compass, so a butterfly flying straight ahead of you flies along that line. Clear removes the direction.

Link a host or nectar plant

Tap Host plant or Nectar plant. A list shows the plant records that fit, with those from this visit first. Tap one, or tap New plant record to create it now.

If you chose the behaviour Oviposition or the life stage Larva, the app suggests linking the host plant. It is only a suggestion, and it disappears once a host plant is linked.

Which plant records can be linked depends on their Relation to butterflies: a host plant link accepts larval host plants, oviposition plants and unknown associations. A nectar plant link accepts nectar sources and unknown associations.

Add photos

Open Details and scroll to Photos.

  • Photo opens the camera. Gallery lets you choose an existing picture.
  • You can add several. The first gets a star, which marks the main photo. The cross removes a photo before you save.
  • Photos are made smaller on the phone (at most 2048 pixels on the long side) and upload during sync.

Species that need a photo. An admin can mark a species as needing a photo. When you choose it, the sheet says This species needs a photo. The record will wait for validation. Add one with Add photo if you can. You can still save without a photo, because the butterfly may have flown off. You can add it later, and the Finish screen reminds you.

Plants and habitat during a walk

The visit screen also has Add plant and Add habitat. Records made here belong to this visit. The forms are explained in Plants and habitat.

TipPlants are not part of the standard count. If stopping would disturb your walk, pause first, or record plants after you finish, from the Plants & habitat tile.

Finish the visit

At the end of the transect, tap Finish. Going back from this screen changes nothing, so you can carry on walking if you tapped it too early.

  1. Check the summary. Does it match what you remember?
  2. Read the warnings. See the table below.
  3. Enter the weather at the end: Temperature, Wind and Cloud cover. If you really could not, say why in If you could not record the weather, say why.
  4. If you saw no butterflies, tick the true-zero box. See below.
  5. Tap Finish and save. Recording stops, and the app tries to sync.

Confirm a true zero

If the visit has no butterfly records, a box appears: No butterfly was seen during the complete standardised visit. Tick it only if you walked the visit properly. You cannot finish a visit with no butterflies without it. If you did not walk it properly, use Cancel visit instead.

Cancel a visit

Use Cancel visit at the bottom of the Finish screen when the walk could not be completed properly. Choose a reason, add a note if useful, and confirm. Back returns without changing anything.

ReasonUse it when
Aborted (weather)You stopped because the weather turned, for example a storm came in.
Aborted (other reason)You stopped for any other reason, for example an injury or the transect was blocked.
Invalid (protocol not followed)You walked, but not by the method, for example the wrong route.

The butterflies you recorded before cancelling are kept, but the visit does not count as valid. A cancelled visit cannot be restarted. Start a new one if you walk the transect again.

Record a visit that did not happen

If a planned visit did not take place at all, you can record that. Missed visits are useful information too.

  1. Open the start form for the transect.
  2. Tap Not conducted… at the top right.
  3. Add a note if useful and tap Record as not conducted.

After the walk: review and correct

Look at a visit

Open Transects, tap the transect, and tap the visit under My visits. A draft reopens the start form, and a visit in progress reopens the visit screen. Any other visit opens this read-only page:

Good to knowRoutes are stored on the phone that recorded them and are sent to the server, but they are not sent back to phones. On another phone, or after removing the app's data, a visit has no route on the map and no route coverage. The server still has it.

To see what each status means, see Visit statuses.

Correct a record

Tap a butterfly in the visit to open it. To change something, tap the pencil at the top right.

  • You can change the species, count and all details. The time and position stay as recorded.
  • You can edit until the visit is locked. On a locked visit the pencil is gone. Ask an admin to unlock it if something must change.
  • Changing the species or confidence of a record that has not yet been decided can send it to validation, or take it out again.
  • The message This observation has a sync problem. Open the sync screen first. means an earlier change to this record is stuck. See Errors.

Manage photos

On a record, the Photos section has Take photo and From gallery. Photos added here are saved right away. Tap a photo to view it full screen, and pinch to zoom. The icons at the top let you make it the main photo (star) or delete it (bin).

A photo from another phone is fetched from the server when you open it, so it needs a connection. Without one you see the small preview if it has already been downloaded.

Coming laterYou will be able to add a forgotten butterfly to a finished visit. Today, Add butterfly exists only while the visit is in progress.

Plants and habitat

Besides butterflies, you can record the plants they depend on and describe the habitat. Open the Plants & habitat tile, or use Add plant and Add habitat during a visit.

  • From the visit screen, the record belongs to that visit.
  • From the tile, the record stands alone, with no visit.

The chips All, Plants and Habitat filter the list. Tap a row to view and change it. You see only your own records.

Coming laterMembers of a project will see each other's plants and habitat features, read-only, so everyone benefits from what the project already knows.

Set a location: point, patch or line

Tap Location to open the map. Choose a method at the top, draw, then tap the tick at the top right to keep it. The back arrow leaves without changing the location. Changing the method clears what you drew, and the app asks first.

MethodHowBest for
PointTap the map to place it, or Use my position.A single plant or small clump. Fastest.
Polygon (tap)Tap the corners in order. Undo removes the last corner, Clear removes all, Close finishes. You need at least three.A patch you can see on the map. Works well after the walk.
Polygon (walk)Start walking, walk along the edge, Stop walking, then Close. A corner is kept about every 2 m. During a visit it uses the visit's GPS, so walk slowly.A larger patch you can walk around.
LineTap points along the line. Habitat features only.A hedge, ditch or path edge.

The screen warns you about shapes that cannot be saved or are unreliable:

  • The edges cross. Undo the last points or start again. The outline crosses itself.
  • The corners are on one line. Add a corner to the side. The shape has no area.
  • This patch is only about … m across, which is too small for GPS. A walked shape this small is unreliable. Use tap mode.
TipA point with an abundance class is much quicker than a polygon. Draw a patch only when its size matters.

Sync

Sync sends your changes to the server and fetches what is new. You rarely need to think about it.

When it happens

  • When you log in or open the app
  • When the app comes back to the foreground
  • Every 15 minutes while the app is open
  • When the phone gets a connection back
  • Right after you finish or cancel a visit
  • When you pull down the project list, or tap Sync now
Good to knowSync does not run while the app is closed. After a field day, open the app with a connection and let it finish.

Sync did not finish. Changes stay on this phone. means there was no connection or the server did not answer. Nothing is lost, and the app tries again later.

Errors: the server refused a change

Sometimes the server refuses a change, for example because an admin locked the visit in the meantime. The change stays on your phone under Errors with the reason.

  • Retry sends it again. Use it once the cause is gone. For a locked visit, that means after an admin has unlocked it.
  • Discard local change removes your change. The server's version comes back on the next sync.

A photo the server refused appears here too, with Try again and Delete photo. See the list of sync messages.

Conflicts: you and an admin changed the same thing

A conflict happens when you change a value offline and someone changes the same value on the server before your change arrives. If you changed different fields, both changes are simply combined.

You cannot resolve a conflict in the app. An admin looks at both versions and decides. Until then, the card says An admin will resolve this. Afterwards it says which version won, with the admin's note.

Save mobile data

In Settings, turn on Upload photos on Wi-Fi only. Records still sync over mobile data, but photos wait for Wi-Fi.

Problems and answers

I logged in but see no projects.

Pull the list down to refresh. If it stays empty, an admin has not added you to a project yet.

A project disappeared from my list.

An admin may have removed you from it. The records you already synced are safe on the server. Ask your admin.

The distance to the transect is wrong.

Tap the location icon on the transect list to take a new position. Indoors, or just after turning on GPS, the position can be poor.

The app does not record my route when the screen is off.

Your phone is probably saving battery too aggressively. In Android's settings for the app, set battery use to Unrestricted, and check that notifications are allowed. Do a short test walk at home.

My route has straight jumps.

Those are GPS gaps, for example from dense forest or a phone deep in a bag. Keep the phone near the top of a pocket or in your hand. Check that battery saver is off.

The battery ran out during a walk.

Everything recorded up to then is safe. Charge the phone and open the app. The visit continues, and the Finish screen shows the gap. If you could not complete the walk, cancel it with a reason.

I forgot to tap Finish.

The visit stays in progress and the app keeps recording. Open it and tap Finish. If the walking time is now much too long, tell your admin, who can correct the times.

I forgot to tap Start and have already walked.

Start the visit now and note what happened in the notes, or walk it again if you can. The app takes the time from the phone, so records cannot be dated back.

I recorded the wrong species.

Within 10 seconds, tap Undo. Later, open the record and tap the pencil, then Change species. See Correct a record.

I forgot a butterfly and the visit is finished.

You cannot add it to a finished visit yet, and neither can an admin. Tell your admin so that there is a note of it. Before you tap Finish and save, you can still go back and add it.

Sync never finishes or always fails.

Check your connection, and try opening a website on the phone. If that works, the server may be off, so ask your admin. Your data stays safe on the phone.

A photo stays "waiting".

If Upload photos on Wi-Fi only is on, connect to Wi-Fi. Otherwise open Unsynced data, tap Sync now and check Errors.

I have a new phone.

Sync the old phone first and check that nothing is waiting. Install the app on the new phone, log in and download your project. Your visits and records come back, but routes do not appear on the new phone's map. They are safe on the server.

Can other people see my records?

Other researchers cannot. Admins can see everything in the projects, to check and export it.
